Works alongside your existing tools

AlgoPharma organizes the case, documents, tasks, deadlines, and follow-up history in one place. It does not replace:

  • Pharmacy management systems (e.g. Kroll, PharmaClik Rx)
  • Insurance adjudication systems
  • Insurer portals
  • Fax, phone, email or electronic submission channels
  • Pharmacist or physician judgment

Special Authorization Case Workspace

Drag cards between stages, or click a card for the full case file — including the coverage result for its drug. The colored risk label on each card shows the biggest active risk; hover it for full details. Patient names are never shown.

AlgoPharma provides insurance coverage information and pharmacy administrative workflow support. It does not provide diagnostic, treatment, prescribing, dosing, medication-switching, or clinical suitability advice. Final clinical decisions are made by the prescribing physician and pharmacist. Final reimbursement decisions are made by the insurer.
